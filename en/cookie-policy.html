Cookie Policy

QR Tool does not set any cookies in your browser. Not to remember your settings, not for statistics and not for marketing.

What we keep in your browser

  • Settings: theme (light or dark), font size, high contrast and whether you have closed the introduction. They live in your browser's localStorage, not in cookies, and never leave your device.
  • QR history: off by default, and only saved to localStorage if you tick the box yourself. Read more in the privacy policy.
  • Offline copy: the site keeps its own files in the browser so it can open quickly again.

Statistics

We use Plausible for anonymous visitor statistics. Plausible uses no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. The statistics count page views and clicks on links to other sites. What you type in and what you scan is not sent to the statistics.

Advertisements

No ads are shown and no ad scripts are loaded. If ads are activated later, we will ask for your consent before anything that requires it is loaded, and this page will be updated.

How to delete it

Delete the site data for qrtool.dk in your browser's settings and everything QR Tool has stored on your device is gone.
