How to scan a QR code

A QR code is read with the camera on a smartphone. Most phones from 2018 onwards can do it straight from the camera app, without installing anything. If you would rather read a code from an image or a screenshot, use the upload field above.

  1. iPhone: Open the Camera app, point it at the QR code, and tap the notification that appears. This works from iOS 11. If you have turned off "Scan QR Codes" under Settings → Camera, turn it back on.
  2. Android: Open the Camera app or Google Lens and point at the code. On older Android versions you can use the Google app and tap the camera icon instead.
  3. From an image: If you have a screenshot or a photo of the code, upload the image here and the content appears instantly.
  4. If the code will not read: Move closer, turn on the light, wipe the lens, and hold the phone still. The sharper the image, the easier the code is to read.

What can a QR code contain?

A QR code is just a link or a piece of text inside an image. When it is read, the scanner shows the content - and if it is a link, you can open it directly. The most common types are:

  • Links and text - to websites, landing pages and short messages.
  • WiFi - network name and password so guests can connect with a single scan.
  • Contact (vCard) - name, phone, email and address saved to the phone book.
  • Email and SMS - ready-made messages that open in the right app.
  • Calendar - events added straight to the calendar.
  • Payments, app links and much more.

Is it safe to scan a QR code?

This scanner reads the code locally in your browser. The image or code is not sent to us or to any third party, and it only shows you the content - it does not automatically open anything you have not tapped yourself.

Still be careful with QR codes from a source you do not know. A QR code can point to any link, so check the address in the result before you open it. If you are unsure about a code, leave it alone.

Frequently asked questions about QR scanning

How do I scan a QR code without an app?

On iPhone, open the Camera app and point it at the code. On Android, open Camera or Google Lens. To read a code from an image or a screenshot, upload the image on this page instead.

Can I scan a QR code from an image or screenshot?

Yes. Tap Upload image and choose the image with the QR code. The code is read in your browser and you get the content instantly.

Does the QR scanner cost anything?

No. It is completely free and requires neither registration nor login.

Is my image or QR code sent to a server?

No. Everything is read locally in your browser and is not sent to us or anyone else - even if the code contains a WiFi password or contact details.

Why can't my QR code be scanned?

It is usually caused by blur, too little light, weak contrast or a damaged code. Try moving closer, turning on the light, wiping the lens and holding the phone still. A sharp screenshot gives the best result.

Can I scan a WiFi QR code here?

Yes. The scanner shows the content of any QR code, including WiFi codes. To join the network, use your phone's camera, which offers to connect right away.
