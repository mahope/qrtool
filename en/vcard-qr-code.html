Best Practices for vCard QR Codes

  • Keep it relevant: Only include the contact information you want to share professionally
  • Always test: Scan your QR code with your own phone to make sure all information is correct
  • Use good contrast: A dark QR code on a light background provides the best scannability
  • Add to business cards: Print the QR code on the back of your physical business card as a supplement
  • Update when changed: Generate a new QR code if your contact information changes

Frequently Asked Questions About vCard QR Codes

What is a vCard QR code?

A vCard QR code contains your contact information in a standardized format. When someone scans the code, they can save your name, phone number, email and more directly to their phone contacts.

Do vCard QR codes work on all phones?

Yes! vCard QR codes are supported by both iPhone (iOS 11+) and Android phones. Most camera apps can scan and interpret vCard data automatically.

What information can I include in a vCard?

You can include name, company, title, phone number, email, website and address. The more information you include, the larger the QR code becomes.

Can I use the vCard QR code on my business card?

Absolutely! Download the QR code in SVG format for the best print quality, and place it on the back of your business card.

Is my contact information secure?

Yes, your information is only processed locally in your browser. No data is sent to a server. The QR code is generated 100% on your own device.
