Why Use SMS QR Codes?

SMS QR codes allow your customers to send pre-filled text messages with a single scan. Instead of asking people to remember a phone number and type a message manually, they can simply scan the code and tap send.

Campaigns

Let customers sign up for newsletters, contests or offers by scanning a QR code and sending a keyword via SMS.

Customer Service

Give customers the ability to quickly contact your support via SMS with a pre-filled subject and description.

Events

Use SMS QR codes for RSVP, ticket registration or event information. Attendees scan and send confirmation with a single tap.

Feedback

Collect customer feedback by letting them send a quick SMS with their opinion directly from a QR code on the receipt.

How It Works

  1. Enter phone number: Type the phone number the SMS should be sent to.
  2. Write message: Add an optional pre-filled message that the recipient can send.
  3. Generate the QR code: Click the button and your SMS QR code is generated instantly.
  4. Download & share: Download as PNG or SVG and use it in print or digitally.
  5. Scan & send: The recipient scans the code, the SMS app opens with a pre-filled message, and they tap send.

Best Practices for SMS QR Codes

  • Keep the message short: Pre-filled SMS messages should be short and clear for best results
  • Use country code: Always include the country code (e.g. +1) to ensure correct delivery
  • Add instructions: Write "Scan to send SMS" so people know what the QR code does
  • Test on both platforms: Make sure to test the QR code on both iPhone and Android
  • Place visually: Use the QR code in print, on posters, business cards or packaging

Frequently Asked Questions About SMS QR Codes

Do SMS QR codes work on iPhone?

Yes! When an iPhone scans an SMS QR code, the Messages app opens automatically with the pre-filled phone number and message. The user simply needs to tap send.

Does it work on Android phones?

Yes, Android phones open the default SMS app with the pre-filled number and message, ready to send.

Does it cost anything to send SMS via QR code?

The QR code itself is free to generate. However, the SMS that is sent is charged according to the recipient's normal SMS rate from their carrier.

Can the recipient edit the message before sending?

Yes, the message is only pre-filled. The recipient can freely edit the text in the SMS app before tapping send.

How many characters can the message contain?

We recommend keeping the message under 160 characters for best compatibility. Longer messages may work but can be split into multiple SMS messages.
