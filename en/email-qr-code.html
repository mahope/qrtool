Why Use Email QR Codes?

Email QR codes make it incredibly easy for customers, guests and contacts to send emails to you. Instead of typing an email address manually, they can simply scan a QR code and get a pre-filled email ready to send.

Customer Service

Make it easy for customers to contact your support. Place the QR code on products, receipts or in-store.

Marketing

Add email QR codes to flyers, posters and business cards. Increase the number of inquiries with pre-filled emails.

Events

Let attendees register, give feedback or ask questions by scanning a QR code on-site.

Support

Place the QR code on hardware, manuals or packaging so users can quickly report issues or request help.

Best Practices for Email QR Codes

  • Keep the subject short: A clear and concise subject makes it easier for the recipient to sort emails
  • Pre-fill the message: Give the user a template they can edit, so they don't start from scratch
  • Place strategically: Put the QR code on business cards, product packaging or in shop windows
  • Test before use: Scan the QR code with your own phone to make sure the email looks correct
  • Add instructions: Write "Scan to send email" so people know what the QR code does

Frequently Asked Questions About Email QR Codes

How does an email QR code work?

When a user scans the QR code, their default email app opens with the recipient, subject and message pre-filled. The user simply needs to press "Send".

Do email QR codes work on both iPhone and Android?

Yes! Email QR codes use the mailto protocol, which is supported by all smartphones. The code opens whichever email app the user has installed.

Can the recipient edit the email before sending?

Yes, the QR code only pre-fills the fields. The user can freely edit the subject, message and add attachments before sending.

Is there a limit to the message length?

QR codes have a capacity limitation. We recommend keeping the total text under 300 characters for optimal scannability.

Can I use email QR codes for newsletters?

Yes, you can create a QR code that pre-fills a subscription email for your newsletter. Set the subject as "Subscribe to newsletter" and a short message.
