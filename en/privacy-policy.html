Privacy Policy

Updated 20 November 2026.

We take your privacy seriously. This page describes what information is processed and what choices you have. The service is designed to work without collecting personal data beyond what is necessary to deliver the service.

What is stored locally

Settings such as theme, font size and your QR code history are stored in your browser's localStorage. QR Tool itself sets no cookies. This data does not leave your device.

QR history

QR history is off by default. New QR codes are only saved to your browser's localStorage when you explicitly opt in via the checkbox - the choice applies to your current visit only and resets on reload or navigation. Once opted in, the first 200 characters of each newly generated code are stored (up to 20 non-favorites plus favorites). Unchecking stops future saves but does not delete existing entries. The "Clear history" button deletes the entire history.

History entries can contain sensitive information, such as WiFi passwords from WiFi QR codes or contact details from vCard codes. Avoid enabling history on shared or public devices, and clear the history when you are done.

Analytics

If visitor statistics are used, they are cookie-free and anonymised. No personally identifiable data is processed.

Advertisements

QR Tool uses Google AdSense for ads. For that reason the pages load a script from Google (pagead2.googlesyndication.com). No ads are shown until they are switched on, but the script is already loaded so that Google can show the consent dialog.

If you are in the EEA, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google shows a consent dialog (Google's "Privacy & messaging") the first time you visit. There you choose whether Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies for ads and measurement. If you say no, only ads that do not rely on your data can be shown. You can change your choice later through the button or link that Google's dialog shows on the page, or by deleting the site data for qrtool.dk in your browser.

Google is responsible for the data it collects for ads. Read more in Google's explanation of how it uses data from ads and Google's privacy policy.

What you type into a QR code is not sent to Google and does not appear in the page address.

See also the cookie policy.

Your rights

You can delete local data at any time via your browser's settings. Contact us with questions: kontakt@mahope.dk.
