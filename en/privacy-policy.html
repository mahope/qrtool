Privacy Policy

We take your privacy seriously. This page describes what information is processed and what choices you have. The service is designed to work without collecting personal data beyond what is necessary to deliver the service.

What is stored locally

Settings such as theme, cookie consent and tool preferences may be stored in your browser's localStorage or cookies. This data does not leave your device.

Analytics

If visitor statistics are used, they are cookie-free and anonymised. No personally identifiable data is processed.

Advertisements

We may apply for Google AdSense. Before approval, no ad scripts are displayed. When ads are activated, a consent banner will be shown, and no personal data is shared before consent.

Your rights

You can delete local data at any time via your browser's settings. Contact us with questions: kontakt@mahope.dk.
