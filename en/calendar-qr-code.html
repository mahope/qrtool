Why Use Calendar QR Codes?

Calendar QR codes make it incredibly easy to share events. Instead of asking attendees to manually create an event, they can scan a QR code and get all the details added directly to their calendar - with title, time, location and description.

Conferences

Let attendees scan and add sessions to their calendar. Perfect for multi-day conferences with many agenda items.

Weddings

Include a QR code in the invitation so guests can easily save the date, time and location in their calendar.

Courses & Workshops

Share course schedules via QR code so participants never forget the next session.

Corporate Events

Make it easy for colleagues to add meetings, team building and social events to their calendar.

How It Works

  1. Enter event details: Fill in the title, location, start and end time for your event.
  2. Add description: Give attendees additional information about the event.
  3. Generate the QR code: Click the button and your calendar QR code is generated instantly.
  4. Download & share: Download as PNG or SVG and use it in invitations, posters or online.
  5. Attendees scan: When people scan the code, the event is automatically added to their calendar.

Best Practices for Calendar QR Codes

  • Be precise with time: Double-check start and end times to avoid confusion
  • Include full address: Write the complete address in the location field so attendees can navigate there
  • Add contact info: Include the organizer's contact details in the description
  • Test before distribution: Always scan your QR code to verify that all details are correct
  • Use in invitations: Place the QR code on physical and digital invitations for easy calendar addition

Frequently Asked Questions About Calendar QR Codes

Do calendar QR codes work on iPhone?

Yes! When you scan a calendar QR code with your iPhone, it will automatically offer to add the event to your Apple Calendar.

Does it work with Google Calendar?

Yes, Android phones will offer to add the event to Google Calendar or another calendar app you have installed.

What format does the calendar QR code use?

The QR code contains data in iCalendar format (VCALENDAR/VEVENT), which is the universal standard for calendar events. It works with all modern calendar apps.

Can I include a location?

Yes, you can add a location that will be displayed in the calendar event. Use a full address so attendees can easily find their way.

Is there a limit to the description length?

QR codes have a capacity limitation, so we recommend keeping the description short and concise - typically under 200 characters for best scanning results.
