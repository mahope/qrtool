My Mission

QR Tool was created with a simple vision: To make professional QR code generation accessible to everyone - completely free, without limitations or hidden fees.

In a world where many QR code generators charge for basic features, I wanted to create a tool that gives you full control and all features without having to pay.

What Makes QR Tool Special?

Modern Design

Choose between different QR styles (squares, rounded, dots) and customise colours to match your brand.

WiFi QR Codes

Let your guests connect to WiFi with a single scan - perfect for restaurants, hotels and events.

Digital Business Cards

Generate vCard QR codes that automatically save contact information to the phone.

Batch Generation

Need many QR codes? Generate up to 100 at once and download as a ZIP file.

Privacy First

All QR codes are generated locally in your browser. I never send your data to servers. Only anonymised statistics via Plausible.

QR History

Your latest 20 QR codes are stored locally on your device for easy access.

Features in Detail

QR Code Types

  • Text/URL: Standard QR codes for websites, landing pages, or plain text
  • WiFi: Automatic WiFi connection with SSID, password and encryption type
  • vCard: Digital business cards with name, phone, email, website and address
  • Email: Pre-filled email messages with recipient, subject and body
  • SMS: Pre-written SMS messages to specific numbers
  • Calendar: iCalendar events that can be added directly to the calendar

Design Options

  • 3 QR Styles: Classic squares, rounded corners, or modern dots
  • Colour Customisation: Choose any colour for the QR code and background
  • Transparent Background: Perfect for placing QR codes on coloured surfaces
  • Multiple Sizes: 256px to 2048px - choose the right size for your needs
  • Error Correction: 4 levels (L, M, Q, H) for optimal scannability

Download Formats

  • PNG: Standard format, perfect for web and print
  • SVG: Vector-based format that scales perfectly to any size
  • JPG: Compressed format for smaller file size
  • WebP: Modern format with optimal compression for web

Why is QR Tool Free?

That is a fair question when most competitors charge for their services. Here is my philosophy:

  1. Open Source Spirit: I believe in sharing knowledge and tools with the community.
  2. Client-side Technology: Since everything happens in your browser, I have almost no server costs.
  3. Portfolio Project: QR Tool demonstrates my skills in web development.
  4. Passion for Quality: I enjoy creating tools that people love to use.
  5. Community Contribution: By being free, I can help small businesses, students and individuals.

No Hidden Fees: QR Tool will always be 100% free. No premium plans, no limitations, no tricks.

The Technology Behind QR Tool

QR Tool is built with modern web technologies for optimal performance and security:

Frontend

  • Vanilla JavaScript - No framework overhead
  • Modern CSS with CSS Variables
  • Responsive Design - Works on all devices
  • Dark/Light Mode support

Security & Privacy

  • 100% client-side - No QR code data is sent to servers
  • HTTPS encryption
  • Security headers (CSP, X-Frame-Options)
  • Anonymised analytics (Plausible) - no cookies

Performance

  • Optimised CSS and JavaScript
  • Gzip compression
  • Browser caching
  • CDN for external libraries
