Why Use WiFi QR Codes?

WiFi QR codes revolutionize the way guests connect to wireless networks. Instead of spelling out long, complex passwords, your guests can simply scan a QR code and be connected in seconds.

Hotels & Airbnb

Place the WiFi QR code in the rooms and give guests instant access without having to call the front desk.

Restaurants & Cafes

Let your guests connect to WiFi while waiting for their food. Significantly improves the customer experience.

Offices & Meeting Rooms

Give visitors and consultants easy access to guest WiFi without compromising security.

Personal Use

When you have guests at home, you no longer need to dictate long passwords.

How It Works

  1. Enter network info: Type your WiFi network name (SSID) and password.
  2. Choose encryption: Most modern networks use WPA/WPA2.
  3. Generate the QR code: Click the button and your WiFi QR code is generated instantly.
  4. Download & print: Download as PNG or SVG and print it out.
  5. Place visually: Put the QR code where guests can easily scan it.

Best Practices for WiFi QR Codes

  • Place strategically: Put the QR code at the entrance, at the table or in the rooms
  • Use lamination: Protect your printed QR code with lamination for longer durability
  • Add instructions: Write "Scan for WiFi" so people know what the QR code does
  • Test regularly: Make sure the QR code works after changes to WiFi settings
  • Update when changed: If you change your WiFi password, generate a new QR code

Frequently Asked Questions About WiFi QR Codes

Do WiFi QR codes work on iPhone?

Yes! WiFi QR codes work on iOS 11 and later. The iPhone will automatically show a notification when the QR code is scanned.

Does it work on Android phones?

Yes, Android has built-in support for WiFi QR codes from Android 10 onwards. Older versions can use third-party apps.

Is it safe to share my WiFi password via QR code?

Yes, but be mindful of where you place the QR code. For public locations, we recommend setting up a separate guest network.

Can I change my WiFi password later?

If you change your WiFi password, you need to generate a new QR code with the new password. The old QR code will no longer work.

How do I scan a WiFi QR code?

Open the camera app on your smartphone and point it at the QR code. A notification will appear - tap it to connect.
